Figure 1. Two stacked line plots sharing a time axis from 0 to 0.3, for unsteady Stokes flow in a lid-driven cavity, divided into three phases: (a) lid started, up to t of about 0.046; (b) stationary, shaded, up to t = 0.2; (c) lid stopped, after t = 0.2. Top panel: dissipation and lid power. Both start very high at the impulsive start, decrease monotonically and merge onto a dashed line at the Helmholtz minimum of about 3.38, stay there through phase (b), then at t = 0.2 the lid power drops to zero while the dissipation spikes and decays to zero. Bottom panel: kinetic energy rises from zero to a dashed stationary value of about 0.019 during phase (a), stays constant through phase (b), and decays to zero after the lid stops.

Figure 2. Diagram of three boxes side by side, each representing a regime of a dissipative system. (a) Open, nonstationary: external power enters at the top and entropy leaves at the bottom; the rate of free energy plus dissipation equals the external power, with nonzero free energy rate; no extremum of the dissipation when driven through prescribed rates. (b) Open, stationary: power enters and entropy leaves; internal energy and entropy are constant and the dissipation equals the external power; the dissipation is minimized over driving-constrained fields (Prigogine's minimum entropy production). (c) Closed, relaxing: no external work, heat leaves to a bath; the rate of free energy plus dissipation is zero with decreasing free energy; the dissipation is maximized over balance-consistent rates and the Onsager law follows.
